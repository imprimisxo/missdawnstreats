Her Story

Made With Family in Mind

Miss Dawn started making caramel popcorn for her own children and grandchildren.

Her husband encouraged her to make it official. She started sharing it with friends, and their feedback was enthusiastic enough that she decided to offer it to everyone.

She still makes every batch by hand, in small quantities, the same way she started at home.
